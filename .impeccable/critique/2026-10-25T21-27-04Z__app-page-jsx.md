## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Category-interchangeable. Remove the three product images and the layout could front a crypto wallet or an AI lab: a centred wordmark over a faded circuit logo, a tagline band between hairlines, three alternating image rows, and two frosted-glass cards with a typewriter. Only the product imagery and two lines of copy ("Worn, not carried.", "Close to the body.") are authored for this brand, and none of that material shapes the layout, type or motion.

**Deterministic scan:** 2 findings and 1 advisory across three scans (source, 1440x900, 390x844). `overused-font` at app/globals.css:21 (Arial computed on every body paragraph): true positive. `buried-raster` on the logo watermark (EntryPage.jsx:14-18): the logo's shape does reach the screen through its glow, at about 1.05-1.07:1, so the detector's literal claim is a false positive, but the design review reaches the same conclusion in effect: a 241 KB, 2080 px PNG preloaded for a mark nobody can see. `straggler` advisory at 390 px ("Engineer" alone on a line): true positive. The detector did not see the hierarchy, imagery, dead-link or typewriter problems; the design review did. The detector agent additionally measured that the fixed logo layer paints over the lower sections and dims their text to about 0.9 of its value, which the design review confirmed on the lab image.

**Visual overlays:** the in-page detector was injected in a headless browser and logged the same findings; no user-visible overlay exists in this environment.

## Overall Impression

Calm and dark, which suits the brand, but it reads as a holding page assembled from category defaults. The single biggest opportunity: let the product's own material (close to the body, layered, quiet) drive type, light and motion, and make the one action work.

## What's Working

- Restraint: one near-black field, no feature grid, no competing buttons; text contrast never falls below 7:1 and nothing overflows at any tested size.
- The layered-material render is the most specific, premium image on the page.
- Two lines with a real voice: "Worn, not carried." and "Close to the body."

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The first screen does not say what Grovillon is, and hierarchy is inverted.** The only definition is the smallest text on the page and sits below the fold. Fix: move the approved one-sentence description into the first viewport at statement size, let the next section peek above the fold. Command: /impeccable clarify.
- **[P1] Default typography.** Oswald plus system Georgia and Arial; five font files preloaded, four unused families imported. Fix: one self-hosted family with a four-step role scale, no italics for body. Command: /impeccable typeset.
- **[P1] Imagery breaks the premium impression.** An opaque white neck on the garment cut-out, a hard bottom crop, and a bright, stock-like lab scene whose tablet readout reads as a product demonstration. Fix: ghost the neck, fade the hem into the ground, retire the lab scene, treat all images under one light. Command: /impeccable polish.
- **[P1] The hiring call is a dead end.** The address is a span; the page has zero focusable elements. Fix: real mailto links (one per role with a subject), a list, a top-level link to the roles, a closing footer. Command: /impeccable polish.
- **[P2] The typewriter costs more than it gives.** Not in the server HTML, ignores reduced motion, shifts layout. Fix: server-render the passage, reveal once without layout shift, static under reduced motion. Command: /impeccable animate.
