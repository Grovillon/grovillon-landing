## Overall Impression

A disciplined, quiet page with one genuinely authored idea, the lamp, carried through type colour, media treatment and motion. The biggest opportunity is to let that lamp meet the product, and to make the page's single conversion (writing an email) work for visitors without a mail app.

## What's Working

- One lamp, used everywhere: a custom shader rather than a gradient or particle preset, whose tone also drives the focus ring, selection and media fall-off; it stops drawing when settled or off screen.
- Disciplined type and colour: Archivo alone with width as the expressive axis; contrast measured at 16.5, 9.45 and 6.15:1 on the ground, worst body pixel 5.12:1 over the lit relief.
- Correct degradation: all content without script, CPU-only WebGL refused, complete reduced-motion behaviour, a visible ring on all 9 keyboard stops, no overflow from 320 to 2560 px, about 304 KB transferred on desktop, every approved word kept.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The only conversion is a `mailto:` link with no cue and no fallback.** The hero button shows no address and its → reads as navigation; the address is printed only in the last section. Fix: put the approved address on screen in the first viewport, mark links that leave the page, keep the mailto links; a copy-address control needs new, client-approved words. Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P2] Distinctiveness rests on one WebGL layer over a stock skeleton; the relief never meets the product.** Fix: let the lamp's resting place light the garment in the first screen, or give the garment one full-bleed lit moment, keeping the type system. Command: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Without WebGL the signature disappears, and shared links have no image.** Both relief areas fall back to 7% and 5% glows that read as flat black; there is no `og:image`. Fix: a still render of the lit relief as the fallback background, and a 1200x630 share card from the same render. Command: /impeccable harden.
- **[P2] The band's columns stutter and the passage ignores its sentences.** "Built for high-demand conditions." and "Built for situations…" start on the same line, and all seven passage lines have equal gaps though the text is 1 + 4 + 2 lines. Fix without changing words: stack the heading above the passage and space by sentence. Command: /impeccable typeset.
- **[P3] The proof of substance is the smallest, dimmest thing on the page,** followed by 320 px of empty ground. Fix: enlarge the layers render, lighten its treatment, tighten the run into the band. Command: /impeccable bolder.
